# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who uses or contributes to the
TradeSmart SDKs.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks, in any form or venue connected
  to the project.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Keep discussion constructive and on topic.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or close any contribution that breaks these standards, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so repeatedly or seriously.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to TradeSmart at `support@tradesmartonline.in`. Reports are
handled confidentially. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
